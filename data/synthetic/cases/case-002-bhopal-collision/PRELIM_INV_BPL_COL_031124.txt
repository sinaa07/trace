PRELIMINARY ACCIDENT REPORT
Reference: BPL/COL/2024/1103
Date of report: 04-Nov-2024 02:15 hrs
Reporting officer: SI Meena Devi, RPF Bhopal

INCIDENT SUMMARY
At approximately 21:15 hrs on 03-Nov-2024, passenger train 12139 (Bhopal-Nagpur Express)
collided with stationary goods train 53201 in Bhopal Yard at km 4.2 near signal BPL-YD-12.

ON-GROUND SCENARIO
- Type: Rear-end collision (passenger into stationary freight)
- Stationary train: Goods 53201 (empty BOXN rake), stabled on Yard Loop 2 for brake testing since 19:45 hrs
- Impact speed estimated 60-65 km/h based on damage and telemetry
- Passenger rake: 18 coaches + WAP-7 loco; leading coach (S1) crushed, 3 coaches derailed
- Freight rake: 2 wagons damaged at rear, loco undamaged

CASUALTIES
- Passengers: 2 confirmed dead (bodies at Hamidia Hospital), 12 injured (8 admitted, 4 treated OPD)
- Railway staff on 12139: Assistant Driver injured (fractured ribs); Guard injured (head trauma)
- Driver of 12139: NOT PRESENT at scene when rescue arrived at 21:25 hrs
- Driver located at 23:40 hrs at crew rest room, BPL; appeared disoriented, medical exam ordered
- Staff on 53201: Guard present, uninjured; no loco crew (train was stabled)

DRIVER STATUS
- Driver Sh. Rajesh Yadav - ABSENT from cab at time of first responder arrival
- Found later; claims "does not remember applying brakes"
- Breathalyser: Pending (sample collected 00:10 hrs on 04-Nov)
- Driver's emergency brake handle found in applied position in cab

PRELIMINARY OBSERVATIONS
- CTC export shows signal BPL-YD-12 GREEN at 21:14:22 for train 12139
- Separate interlocking panel log obtained from yard cabin shows BPL-YD-12 RED at 21:14:22
- DISCREPANCY between CTC log and interlocking log for same timestamp - under investigation
- No TPWS/ATP intervention recorded
- Stationary train 53201 had handbrake applied; shunting staff confirmed stabling at 19:52 hrs

ACTIONS TAKEN
- Rescue and medical evacuation
- NDRF called off at 01:30 hrs (all passengers accounted)
- Both signal logs seized; CTC workstation imaged
- Driver suspended pending inquiry
- CRS notification sent

Report status: PRELIMINARY - conflicting signal evidence noted
