PRELIMINARY ACCIDENT REPORT
Reference: SML/BRK/2024/0622
Date of report: 22-Jun-2024 15:45 hrs
Reporting officer: ASI G.S. Rawat, GRP Kalka

INCIDENT SUMMARY
At approximately 11:40 hrs on 22-Jun-2024, narrow-gauge passenger train 52459 (Kalka-Shimla)
experienced uncontrolled acceleration and runaway on the 1:25 gradient section between Barog
and Solan at km 46.1-47.2. Train stopped after emergency brake application and sand deployment.

ON-GROUND SCENARIO
- Type: Runaway / brake failure on descending gradient (NOT derailment)
- All 7 coaches and ZDM-3 loco remained on track
- Train halted 200m before SLF-GR-02 signal at km 47.2
- Gradient: 1 in 25 (4%) descending; speed rose from 18 to 58 km/h in ~90 seconds
- No collision with another train or obstruction

CASUALTIES
- Passengers: 45 on board; all evacuated safely by 12:30 hrs; 3 treated for anxiety/ minor bruises at Solan Civil Hospital
- Driver (Sh. Amar Singh): PRESENT but INJURED - trapped in cab for 25 mins, compound fracture left leg, conscious
- Assistant Driver (Sh. Rakesh Kumar): PRESENT, uninjured, assisted evacuation
- Guard (Sh. Suresh Chand): PRESENT, uninjured

DRIVER STATUS
- Driver PRESENT at scene throughout. Rescued from cab by fire brigade at 12:05 hrs.
- Driver statement: "Applied brakes at Barog departure, felt normal initially. After crossing
  km 46.5 brakes felt spongy. Applied emergency brake and sand - train slowed but took long distance"
- Driver fit for duty certificate expired 15-Jun-2024 (renewal pending)

PRELIMINARY OBSERVATIONS
- Telemetry shows brake_status=RELEASED continuously during acceleration phase (11:38:02 - 11:39:28)
- Emergency brake applied at 11:39:40; speed dropped from 58 to 0 km/h over ~28 seconds
- Depot maintenance records show brake inspection OVERDUE since 20-Jun; pre-departure test marked PASSED same morning
- Signal SLF-GR-01 was GREEN; no signal violation
- Track and rolling stock brake blocks to be examined at workshop

ACTIONS TAKEN
- Driver extricated and hospitalised
- Passengers bussed to Shimla
- Train secured at site; traffic suspended Kalka-Solan until 18:00 hrs
- Mechanical branch directed to inspect entire rake brake rigging
- Commissioner of Railway Safety (Safety) notified

Report status: PRELIMINARY - maintenance records requested from Kalka depot
