PRELIMINARY ACCIDENT REPORT
Reference: KNP/DRL/2024/0814
Date of report: 14-Aug-2024 09:30 hrs
Reporting officer: SI R.K. Sharma, GRP Kanpur

INCIDENT SUMMARY
At approximately 04:48 hrs on 14-Aug-2024, goods train 18447 (CNB-LKO freight)
derailed at km 142.8 near KNP Curve Section between Ishwariganj and Fatehpur
on the Kanpur-Lucknow main line.

ON-GROUND SCENARIO
- Type: Derailment (8 wagons off track, 3 overturned)
- Track condition: Wet due to overnight rain; no visible track defect at point of derailment
- Train consist: 42 loaded wagons (coal), 2 brake vans, WDG-4 loco #12341
- Point of derailment: Up main line, left-hand curve R=580m, permanent speed restriction 45 km/h

CASUALTIES
- Passengers: N/A (freight train)
- Railway staff: Driver (Sh. Vijay Kumar) - present at scene, conscious, lacerations on forehead and left arm, shifted to UHM Hospital Kanpur
- Assistant Driver (Sh. Mohan Lal) - present, uninjured
- Guard (Sh. Pradeep Singh) - present, minor bruises
- Public: None reported

DRIVER STATUS
- Driver PRESENT at scene. Did not leave. Statement recorded at 06:15 hrs.
- Driver reported "felt sudden lateral jerk, applied emergency brake immediately"
- Breathalyser: Negative (conducted 05:40 hrs)

PRELIMINARY OBSERVATIONS
- Skid marks visible over ~180m before point of derailment
- Last known telemetry shows speed 92-94 km/h approaching curve (restriction 45 km/h)
- Signal KNP-S42 was GREEN at 04:46:40; train entered curve section under clear signal
- evidence of track sabotage as well. found possible traces of object based hindrance.
- RPF and engineering team on site; traffic suspended on Up line

ACTIONS TAKEN
- Medical aid to driver
- Site secured
- Divisional inquiry ordered
- Black box / event recorder custody chain initiated

Report status: PRELIMINARY - subject to Commissioner of Railway Safety inquiry
